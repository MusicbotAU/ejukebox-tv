The fifteen minutes

What a demo actually looks like.

There is no pitch deck and no discovery workshop. You tell us about the room, we put your name on a screen, and you watch the photo wall and the digital signage run together on it.

01 - Talk

A call, or we come in

Fifteen minutes on the phone about your room - how many screens, where they hang, what is on them now, and whether functions are a big part of what you do. If you are close enough, we would rather come in and see it.

02 - Watch

Your room on the screen

We run a live screen with your logo, your specials and your name on it. Take a photo on your own phone and watch it land on the screen a few seconds later. That is the bit that does the selling for us.

03 - Let it run

See what your customers do

Leave it going for a bit and watch what happens on a normal Thursday. People work the QR code out on their own - that is what the prompts are there for.

What to tell us

  • The venue and the town. Pub, club, bar, cafe, function room - and where it is.
  • How many screens. Including the one in the corner nobody has switched on since the last grand final.
  • What is on them now. Sport, a slideshow off a USB stick, a black screen, or nothing at all.
  • When to ring. Before the lunch rush, after close, Tuesday afternoon - whatever suits the room.

On the day

What we would need from you.

Short list, and none of it is a project.

  • A spare HDMI port. On the TV you already have. The player is a compact box that plugs straight into it.
  • Wifi it can reach, or a network point near the TV. The network point is the sturdier of the two if the screen is a long way from the office.
  • Your logo. Whatever file you have. If the only copy is on a coaster, tell us and we will sort it out.
  • About ten minutes of somebody's time. On the day it goes in. Not ten minutes a week afterwards - it looks after itself once it is running.

After you say yes

What happens next.

Three steps, and only one of them is yours.

01 - We set it up

Configured before it leaves

Your logo, your first slides, your schedule and your team's sign-ins are all set up remotely at our end. Nothing arrives as a box of parts and a manual.

02 - It arrives ready

One box, one cable

The player turns up already knowing which venue it belongs to and what it is meant to be showing. There is no setup wizard and no account to create on a TV remote.

03 - Plug it in

It starts, and keeps starting

Into the spare HDMI port. It comes up with your room on it, powers on with the venue every day after that, and from then on you drive it from your phone.
