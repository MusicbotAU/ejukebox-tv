What this is: everything the room made on the night, handed back to the person whose night it was. Guest photos off the wall, the voice dedications people recorded with their song requests, and the album that played between them. Reachable only by its URL. `noindex: true` writes the robots meta tag, `sitemap: false` keeps it out of sitemap.xml, and it is deliberately linked from no menu - the address is the key. The address is also unguessable on purpose (the hex suffix), because there is no login here. The photos live in the `events` container on the ejukeboxdisplays storage account, copied there from the venue's own slideshow container so that resetting the venue screen cannot take them away. Everything on the page is driven by manifest.json next to them, so re-running the event export updates the page with no edit here. WHY THE PHOTOS ARE NOT TAGS (Marc, 13 Sep 2026: "disable the right clicking... the whole point is to bring people to our site"). They are background images on a div with a transparent shield over the top, so the browser's own "Save image as" and "Copy image" never appear, dragging one to the desktop does nothing, and iOS long-press does not offer to save. A determined person can still screenshot, and nothing in a browser can stop that - this raises the effort enough that the easy path is to come back to the page, which is the point. The photos also carry the venue and eJukebox branding baked in by the watermarker, so a screenshot travels with the brand on it. {%- endcomment -%}
