Club Buffalo · Saturday 12 September 2026

Riley’s 21st.

Everything the room made that night — the photos that went up on the screen as they were taken, and the dedications people recorded for each other.

The night Photos Dedications The album ↑ Top

How it went

A room that picked its own music.

The first request landed at nineteen minutes past eight. Cotton Eye Joe — somebody had to.

From there until twenty to midnight, thirty-three songs went up, and every one of them was chosen by somebody standing in the room. Hilltop Hoods into Tone Loc into Wu-Tang, which is not an order any DJ would have written down beforehand, and exactly the order a room full of people actually wanted.

The photos kept pace. Eighty of them were up on the big screen before the night was out, the first at eight minutes past six and the last just before eleven, most of them on the wall within seconds of being taken.

And three people did the thing hardly anybody does. They picked a song, recorded a message to go with it, and the whole room heard their voice just before the track dropped. Those three are on this page, exactly as they went out.

None of it came off a playlist written in advance. It was just what the room wanted next, all night.

Happy 21st, Riley.
