The moment that matters

Photo taken to photo on the wall: about three seconds.

Fast enough that the guest is still holding their phone and looking at the screen when their own face lands on it. That is when the rest of the room turns around, and that is the bit no other screen in the building does.

PIN-locked player
  • The TV is PIN-locked. Walk up to the screen, press Escape, and you get a PIN pad - not a desktop, not a file manager, not somebody's browser tabs.
  • It heals itself. A watchdog restarts the display if anything ever stops it, so a hiccup at 9pm on a Saturday does not become a black screen for the rest of the night.
  • We watch the fleet. Players are monitored and managed remotely, so problems get spotted and fixed at our end rather than found by your staff.
  • Approve or hide from your pocket. The manager app handles photos one at a time or in bulk, fires a slide, changes the schedule, and starts or stops the show from anywhere.
  • Push notifications. The right people get told when something actually needs a decision. A glance at the phone, one tap, done.
  • Sign-in without passwords. Staff get in with Google, Microsoft or Apple, organised into per-venue teams. Add a manager by email, remove someone the day they leave.
  • Powers on with the venue. A compact player into any spare HDMI port. It starts when the room starts and runs around the clock with nobody touching it.
  • Australian owned and supported. Built here, run here, and answered by a person on an Australian phone number.
